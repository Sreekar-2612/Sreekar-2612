### 👨‍💻 Professional About Me

I am a software engineer and computer science researcher at **IIITDM Kancheepuram** (Indian Institute of Information Technology, Design and Manufacturing), specializing in **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**, **Production Generative AI Systems**, and **High-Performance Software Development**.

My engineering philosophy centers on **first-principles systems architecture**:
- **Applied AI & Deep Learning:** Engineering robust neural pipelines with a primary focus on Vision Transformers (Swin / ViT), multispectral radiometric satellite processing, and clinical early-warning anomaly detection.
- **Generative AI & Agentic RAG:** Architecting enterprise-grade retrieval-augmented generation systems equipped with autonomous tool-calling agents, dense vector embedding indexing, conversational memory, and sub-second hardware acceleration.
- **Full-Stack & Systems Engineering:** Bridging low-level execution (operating system internals, socket programming, memory-centric scheduling) with modern distributed web platforms (React 19, TypeScript, FastAPI microservices, and reactive GIS spatial telemetry).

Whether profiling memory bandwidth bottlenecks in hardware simulators or deploying production containerized web applications, I prioritize typed API boundaries, measurable latency benchmarks, and clean, resilient codebases.
